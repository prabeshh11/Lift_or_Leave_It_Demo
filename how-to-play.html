HOW TO PLAY

⌨

Controls

WASD to walk. Move mouse to look around. SPACE to jump. E to inspect a nearby package. Click the game canvas to lock your mouse for movement.

📦

Inspect Packages

Walk near a glowing package (yellow ring overhead). Press E - the panel opens and your mouse is released so you can choose.

🏋

Choose Your Technique

Read the package details and choose: Solo Lift, Team Lift, or Use Trolley. Watch the technique animation, then move to the next package.

🎯
